KineticClassroom

An iPad app for teachers. It shows, at a glance, whether a student using an under-desk bike is settled, warming up, or over-activated.

Some students focus better when they can move. An under-desk pedal exerciser gives them a quiet way to do that without leaving their seat. A small Bluetooth sensor on the pedals sends pedalling speed to the teacher's iPad, and KineticClassroom turns that into a simple regulation status the teacher can read from across the room.

It is a classroom support tool built by a practising primary school teacher. It is not a fitness app and it is not a medical device.

What a school needs

  • An iPad
  • An under-desk pedal exerciser
  • A standard Bluetooth cadence sensor, such as a Garmin Cadence Sensor 2
