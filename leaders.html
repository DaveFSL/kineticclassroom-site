For principals and learning support leaders

An adjustment you can see working — and show you made.

Your school already makes adjustments for students who find it hard to settle. KineticClassroom turns movement at the desk into a structured adjustment: the student pedals quietly, the teacher sees how they're tracking at a glance, and before-and-after observations show whether it's helping.

Supports your NCCD evidence

A documented, consistent adjustment with teacher observations over time contributes to the evidence schools already collect for the NCCD.

Fits the plans you already write

Targets the goals that already appear in student plans: staying on task, managing impulses, coping with frustration, and holding information in mind.

Low risk for IT and privacy

No accounts, no student logins, no cloud. Data stays on the school's iPad, under the teacher's control.

The Term 3 trial

Five students across three classrooms in a Brisbane primary school. Each teacher completed an observation form before the student started on the bike and again at the end of term.

The forms ask about redirections per lesson, time out of seat, on-task time, how much teacher attention the student needs, and the teacher's confidence keeping them regulated.

Common questions

Does IT need to be involved?
Only to install the app on an iPad. There are no accounts, servers or network settings.
Which year levels?
Upper primary and secondary, including learning support and small-group classes.
What does it cost?
Free during the founding period. Pricing for ongoing use is agreed with each school before its trial ends.
Is it a medical device?
No. It's a classroom support tool for teachers.
